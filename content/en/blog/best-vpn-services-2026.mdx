## VPNs Aren't Just for Tech Nerds Anymore

Checking work email on airport Wi-Fi, streaming your home country's Netflix while traveling, or just keeping your ISP from cataloging every site you visit — VPNs have gone mainstream. The problem? Every VPN claims to be the fastest, most secure, best-value option out there. So I subscribed to five of the top contenders for a full month and tested speeds, streaming access, security policies, and general usability. Here's what I found.

## How I Tested

My baseline was a 1Gbps fiber connection in the US. I tested each VPN connecting to servers in the US, Europe, and Asia, measuring download/upload speeds on Speedtest.net. I also checked Netflix, Disney+, and BBC iPlayer unblocking, verified no-logs claims, and used each app daily across Windows, macOS, and iOS.

## Top 5 VPN Services

### 1. NordVPN — Best Overall

Pricing: $3.59/month (2-year plan), $12.99 month-to-month.

| Feature | NordVPN |
|---------|---------|
| Servers | 6,400+ in 111 countries |
| Protocol | NordLynx (WireGuard), OpenVPN |
| Simultaneous connections | 10 |
| Speed (US → Europe) | Down 680 Mbps / Up 420 Mbps |
| Netflix unblocking | US, UK, Japan confirmed |
| No-logs policy | Independently audited |

NordVPN's in-house NordLynx protocol is the real differentiator. Built on WireGuard, it's lightweight and consistently retained about 70% of my raw connection speed — which on a gigabit line means you're still well above what most people need. Streaming unblocking was rock-solid across Netflix, Disney+, and Hulu throughout the entire month.

The Threat Protection feature is a nice bonus — it blocks ads, trackers, and malicious sites, essentially replacing a standalone ad blocker.

### 2. ExpressVPN — Fastest Raw Speeds

Pricing: $4.99/month (2-year plan), $12.95 month-to-month.

| Feature | ExpressVPN |
|---------|-----------|
| Servers | 3,000+ in 105 countries |
| Protocol | Lightway, OpenVPN |
| Simultaneous connections | 8 |
| Speed (US → Europe) | Down 710 Mbps / Up 450 Mbps |
| Netflix unblocking | Most regions confirmed |
| No-logs policy | Independently audited + RAM-only servers |

If pure speed is your priority, ExpressVPN edges out everyone else. The Lightway protocol connects in 1–2 seconds, and the RAM-only server infrastructure means all data is wiped on every reboot — nothing to hand over even if someone came knocking.

It's pricier than NordVPN and allows fewer simultaneous connections, which is why it lands at #2 overall. But the performance is hard to beat.

### 3. Surfshark — Best Value

Pricing: $2.19/month (2-year plan), $15.45 month-to-month.

| Feature | Surfshark |
|---------|-----------|
| Servers | 3,200+ in 100 countries |
| Protocol | WireGuard, OpenVPN |
| Simultaneous connections | Unlimited |
| Speed (US → Europe) | Down 590 Mbps / Up 380 Mbps |
| Netflix unblocking | Major regions confirmed |
| No-logs policy | Independently audited |

Unlimited simultaneous connections. That's Surfshark's killer feature. One subscription covers every device you own, plus your family's. At $2.19/month on the 2-year plan, it's the cheapest option here by a meaningful margin.

Speeds are a step below Nord and Express, but still more than enough for 4K streaming, gaming, and everyday use. CleanWeb handles ad and tracker blocking.

### 4. ProtonVPN — Best for Privacy

Pricing: $4.49/month (2-year plan), $9.99 month-to-month.

| Feature | ProtonVPN |
|---------|-----------|
| Servers | 4,600+ in 91 countries |
| Protocol | WireGuard, OpenVPN, Stealth |
| Simultaneous connections | 10 |
| Speed (US → Europe) | Down 550 Mbps / Up 350 Mbps |
| Netflix unblocking | Major regions confirmed |
| No-logs policy | Swiss jurisdiction + fully open-source |

ProtonVPN comes from the team behind ProtonMail, and the privacy DNA shows. Every app is open-source. They're headquartered in Switzerland — outside the Five Eyes, Nine Eyes, and Fourteen Eyes intelligence-sharing alliances. Independent security audits happen on a regular cadence.

The free tier is genuinely usable — no speed caps, which is basically unheard of for free VPNs. The Stealth protocol is designed to bypass VPN blocks in restrictive countries like China and Iran.

### 5. Mullvad VPN — Maximum Anonymity

Pricing: €5/month (~$5.50), single flat rate. No discounts, no plans.

| Feature | Mullvad VPN |
|---------|------------|
| Servers | 800+ in 40 countries |
| Protocol | WireGuard, OpenVPN |
| Simultaneous connections | 5 |
| Speed (US → Europe) | Down 520 Mbps / Up 330 Mbps |

| No-logs policy | Verified — police raid found zero data |

Mullvad is a different animal. No email required to sign up — you get an account number and that's it. You can pay with cash mailed in an envelope or cryptocurrency. In 2023, Swedish police executed a search warrant at Mullvad's office and confirmed there was literally no user data to seize.

Streaming unblocking is hit-or-miss, and the server count is smaller. But if privacy is your non-negotiable top priority, Mullvad walks the walk like no one else.

## The Verdict: Pick by Use Case

**Best all-around:** NordVPN. The best balance of speed, security, and price. Hard to go wrong here.

**Fastest speeds:** ExpressVPN. If you have the budget and want peak performance, this is the one.

**Best value + family sharing:** Surfshark. Unlimited devices at the lowest price point. Perfect for households.

**Privacy-first:** ProtonVPN or Mullvad. If privacy matters more than anything else, pick between these two. ProtonVPN for a more polished everyday experience; Mullvad for absolute anonymity.

**Want to try before you buy:** ProtonVPN's free plan. It's the only reputable free VPN with no speed limits.

One practical tip — almost every VPN on this list offers a 30-day money-back guarantee. Subscribe, test it on your actual devices and network, and refund if it doesn't work for you. There's really no reason to commit blind.
