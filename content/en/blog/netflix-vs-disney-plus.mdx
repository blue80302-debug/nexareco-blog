## Netflix Just Got More Expensive — Again

Netflix Standard now costs $15.49/month. Disney+ Standard? $9.99. That's a pretty significant gap, and it raises a fair question: is Netflix actually worth paying 50% more? I alternated between both services for three months, comparing content libraries, video quality, and day-to-day usability. Here's what I found.

## Pricing Breakdown

| Plan | Netflix | Disney+ |
|------|---------|---------|
| Ad-supported | $7.99/mo | $9.99/mo (with ads) |
| Standard | $15.49/mo | $9.99/mo (no ads) |
| Premium | $22.99/mo | $13.99/mo |
| Simultaneous streams (Standard) | 2 | 2 |
| Simultaneous streams (Premium) | 4 | 4 |
| Max quality | 4K HDR + Dolby Atmos | 4K HDR + Dolby Atmos |
| Offline downloads | Yes | Yes |

No matter how you slice it, Netflix is pricier. The Premium tier difference alone is $9/month — that adds up to over $100 a year.

## Content Library

Netflix has roughly 17,000+ titles in its library. Disney+ sits around 2,500, though adding the Star brand content (available in many markets) bumps it closer to 5,000.

Numbers-wise, Netflix wins by a landslide. But the real question is whether there's stuff *you* actually want to watch.

**Where Netflix dominates:**
- Massive original series catalog (Squid Game, Wednesday, The Night Agent)
- International originals from dozens of countries
- Reality shows and documentaries
- Stand-up comedy specials

**Where Disney+ shines:**
- The entire Marvel Cinematic Universe
- Star Wars franchise (The Mandalorian, Ahsoka)
- Pixar and Disney classic animation
- National Geographic documentaries

Here's how I'd describe the difference: Netflix is the service you open when you're thinking "I want to watch *something*." Disney+ is the one you open when you know exactly what you're there for. The sheer volume gap is real and you feel it.

## Original Content Quality

Looking at the biggest releases from 2025–2026:

**Netflix highlights:**
- Squid Game Season 3
- Wednesday Season 2
- The Night Agent Season 3
- Black Mirror Season 7

**Disney+ highlights:**
- Avengers: Doomsday tie-in series
- The Mandalorian & Grogu (theatrical crossover)
- Percy Jackson Season 2
- Expanding international original slate

Netflix wins on genre diversity. Disney+ wins on franchise power. If you're a Marvel or Star Wars fan, skipping Disney+ isn't really an option.

## UI and User Experience

Netflix's recommendation algorithm is still best-in-class. It learns your viewing habits and the suggestions are genuinely useful — I'd say about 7 out of 10 recommendations actually interest me.

Disney+ has a cleaner, more organized interface. Content is sorted by brand — Marvel, Star Wars, Pixar, Disney, National Geographic — which makes browsing intuitive. But the recommendation engine is noticeably weaker. It tends to push new releases rather than personalized picks.

The biggest day-to-day difference I noticed was subtitle and dub support. Netflix offers way more language options for most titles. Disney+ has improved, but there are still gaps.

## For Families

If you have kids, Disney+ is a no-brainer value-wise. Disney animations, Pixar films, and National Geographic educational content give you a huge library of kid-safe material. The Kids profile feature works well too — it's properly locked down.

Netflix has a Kids section, but it's fundamentally an adult-oriented service. For family viewing, Disney+ is the more natural fit.

## Pros and Cons

### Netflix

**Pros:**
- Massive content library with unmatched genre diversity
- Strong original programming across multiple languages
- Best-in-class recommendation algorithm
- Budget ad-supported tier available

**Cons:**
- Most expensive major streaming service
- Frequent price increases
- Account sharing crackdowns in most regions

### Disney+

**Pros:**
- Significantly cheaper at every tier
- Exclusive Marvel, Star Wars, and Pixar content
- Excellent for families with children
- Growing international original slate

**Cons:**
- Smaller overall content library
- Limited adult-oriented content
- Weaker recommendation system
- Some titles lack full subtitle/dub support

## The Verdict

**Pick Netflix if:** you want variety across genres, you enjoy international series, or you're primarily watching solo or as a couple. If you want to always have something new to watch when you open the app, Netflix delivers.

**Pick Disney+ if:** you're a Marvel or Star Wars fan, you have kids, or you want solid streaming without paying a premium. It also works great as a secondary subscription alongside another service.

Honestly, the ideal setup is both. But if I had to choose just one, Netflix's content diversity makes it the more versatile pick for most people. That said, Disney+ is so reasonably priced that adding it as a second subscription is an easy recommendation.
